About me

I am a physician-scientist working at the intersection of global health, health equity, digital health and artificial intelligence, with a focus on practical research and technology for underserved communities in Africa.

My research spans artificial intelligence and digital health, mental health and NCDs, infectious disease, health equity, and global health research capacity. I am particularly interested in AI systems that are culturally grounded, useful in low-resource settings and connected to real health-system needs.

Research

  • Artificial intelligence & digital healthAI-enabled tools, conversational systems and digital approaches for underserved populations.
  • Mental health & NCDsCulturally adapted assessment, prevention, community interventions and AI for mental health.
  • Infectious diseaseNetwork analysis, machine learning, surveillance and population-level responses to infection.
  • Health equity & global healthEvidence, implementation and locally led research across African settings.
