About me

I am a physician-scientist working at the intersection of global health, health equity, digital health, and artificial intelligence. My work focuses on using research and technology to address health challenges in underserved communities, particularly in Africa.

My research spans health equity, mental health and non-communicable diseases, digital health and AI, global health, and infectious disease. I am especially interested in building and evaluating practical AI systems that are culturally grounded, useful in low-resource settings, and connected to real public-health needs.
