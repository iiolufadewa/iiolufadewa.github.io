Research

My research sits at the intersection of clinical medicine, artificial intelligence and global health, with a focus on methods that can work with the languages, data constraints and realities of underserved settings.

Current areas

  • AI, NLP & digital healthBuilding and evaluating practical AI systems for health, including conversational AI, language technologies and digital health tools.
  • Computational mental healthCulturally adapting depression and anxiety assessment and developing AI approaches that recognise how distress is expressed in African languages and contexts.
  • Network epidemiologyUsing contact and transmission networks to understand healthcare-associated infection and identify high-impact intervention points.
  • Machine learning for infectious diseaseRisk prediction, surveillance and modelling approaches for infection in settings where data are sparse or imperfect.
  • Health equity & global healthResearch and implementation focused on populations and settings that are routinely underrepresented in health evidence and technology.

Selected projects

  • GENSCORE

    Fine-tuning large language models for automated depression screening in Nigerian Pidgin English, with a broader programme spanning Nigerian languages and culturally grounded assessment.

    LLMs · NLP · Mental health · Low-resource AI

  • BraveHeart

    A community-led mental-health programme using trained lay counsellors to provide support to young internally displaced people, with implementation in Nigeria and Sierra Leone.

    Mental health · Task-shifting · Community intervention

  • Hospital transmission network

    Network-analysis work examining patients, healthcare personnel and fomites to identify potential drivers of healthcare-associated infection and prioritise infection-control interventions.

    Network analysis · Infection control · Epidemiology

  • SRHIN Research Academy

    A capacity-building programme supporting early-career African researchers through research design, analysis, writing and publication.

    Research capacity · Mentorship · Open science
