ABOUT / PROFILE

A clinician, researcher and systems builder.

Working at the intersection of medicine, public health, technology and community action.

A physician who builds across disciplines.

Dr. Isaac Iyinoluwa Olufadewa is a medical doctor, public health scientist, social entrepreneur and global health researcher. His work brings together clinical insight, epidemiology, technology, community engagement and policy to address health inequities in Nigeria and across Africa.

He is the founder and Executive Director of the Slum and Rural Health Initiative (SRHIN), a Nigerian-founded organisation using advocacy, research and technology to expand access to health information and services for marginalised communities.

His recent research has increasingly focused on responsible AI for mental health, including culturally grounded conversational systems, language-aware screening and the governance of AI in African health systems.
