RESEARCH / AGENDA

Questions first. Technology second.

A research programme built around health problems, local evidence and the systems required to turn findings into impact.

01 · COMPUTATIONAL PSYCHIATRY

AI for culturally grounded mental health

Research exploring NLP, generative AI, conversational systems and language-aware assessment to reduce barriers to mental health support. A central principle is that models should understand how distress is actually expressed in local communities, not simply translate tools designed elsewhere.

02 · DIGITAL HEALTH & EQUITY

Digital health that works beyond the well-resourced centre

Designing health technologies and evidence frameworks for low-resource settings, with attention to accessibility, language, trust, data governance and the realities of community delivery.

03 · INFECTIOUS DISEASE

Modelling outbreaks and strengthening preparedness

Population-health modelling, epidemiology and data-driven approaches to understand disease dynamics, transmission and health-system vulnerabilities.

04 · GLOBAL HEALTH POLICY

Turning evidence into policy and action

Research and commentary on global health systems, eHealth strategy, health equity and the role of community actors in shaping health policy.

05 · SIGNATURE RESEARCH POSITION

AI anchored in African intelligence

The 2026 Lancet Regional Health – Africa publication argues for AI in African mental health that is locally grounded, ethically governed and shaped by lived cultural realities. The work foregrounds community co-creation and people with lived experience as part of responsible innovation.
