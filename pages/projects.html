GENSCORE · AI FOR MENTAL HEALTH

Generative AI for culturally adaptive anxiety & depression screening

GENSCORE explores how generative AI can reinterpret and enhance mental health screening in low-resource contexts. The programme builds on GenPHQ and GenGAD concepts, with Nigerian language, idioms of distress, audio/text data, psychometric validation and safety governance at its core.

Languages in the broader programme: Nigerian Pidgin, Yoruba and Hausa, with further validation pathways planned.

Pilot direction: fine-tuning and evaluation of LLMs for Nigerian Pidgin depression screening. A published pilot reports 432 Pidgin-language audio responses and 94.5% PHQ-9 severity-scoring accuracy for GPT-4.1.

BRAVEHEART AI · CONVERSATIONAL HEALTH

Conversational mental health support for underserved young people

BraveHeart AI is positioned as a culturally and linguistically grounded conversational system supporting mental health education, harm reduction, recovery and referral pathways for Nigerian young adults. The wider BraveHeart programme is rooted in community engagement and lived experience.

SRHIN · COMMUNITY HEALTH

Slum and Rural Health Initiative

SRHIN uses advocacy, research and technology to provide healthcare information and services in Africa. Under Isaac's leadership, the organisation has worked across community health, mental health, infectious disease response, research training and health equity.

Its 2023 annual report recorded 1.6 million people reached since founding, projects in more than 120 communities across Africa, and operations registered in five African countries at that time.
